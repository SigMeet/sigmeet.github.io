How It Works

1. Join Any Session

Launch SigMeet, join meetings, lectures, workshops, or YouTube. It captures audio, screen shares, and cues in real time.

2. AI Processes Locally

Offline pipeline transcribes, summarizes, and reasons instantly. Floating agents provide live subtitles and insights.

3. Extract and Refine

Auto-generates decisions, tasks, and “Talk Bites.” Edit in the Markdown cockpit as needed.

4. Sync and Share

Push notes to Apple Notes or Obsidian. Share boards or toggle cloud for team access.

FAQ

Is my data secure?

Yes. Everything runs locally on your Mac first. Cloud is opt-in only, with full control over your models.

What session types does it support?

Team meetings, lectures, workshops, coaching, YouTube—any audio or screen share works seamlessly.

Does it require an internet connection?

No. Core AI is offline. Use cloud toggles for collaboration or advanced models when desired.

How do I get my notes out?

Auto-syncs to Apple Notes or Obsidian. Export “Talk Bites” as podcasts or share boards directly.

Is this the full version?

Yes. SigMeet is fully activated and ready to use—no purchases or subscriptions needed.
